Flamingos are famous for their long, elegant legs and striking pink feathers, which come from pigments in the food they eat. They often gather in large groups in shallow lakes and lagoons, where they feed by scooping up small organisms with their uniquely curved beaks.

These birds are highly social and can be seen standing on one leg for long periods, a behavior that helps conserve body heat. Flamingos are also known for their synchronized movements and loud, honking calls, making them one of the most recognizable birds in the world.

Windows are essential architectural features that allow natural light, ventilation, and views to connect the indoors with the outdoors while also shaping the overall appearance of a building. In homes, offices, and public spaces, they can make a room feel brighter, larger, and more welcoming, or they can create a sense of privacy, enclosure, and calm depending on their size, placement, and design. Historically, windows were simple openings in walls, but modern versions are engineered with advanced glass, framing materials, and sealing systems that improve insulation and reduce energy loss. Large picture windows can capture sweeping landscapes, while narrow casement or awning styles may emphasize ventilation and airflow.
